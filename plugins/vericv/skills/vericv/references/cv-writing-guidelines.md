# CV writing guidelines

## Summary

Write a concise, fact-based summary. Include only documented domain, role, tools, and outcomes. Avoid superlatives such as `expert`, `proven`, or `award-winning` unless source evidence supports them.

## Experience and projects

Use concise action + object + context/outcome bullets. Preserve responsibility scope. Keep original metrics exactly; never manufacture a numeric result. Reorder bullets for relevance but do not move a project into employment or make it appear client work.

## Skills

List technologies, methods, and tools explicitly documented in the CV/profile. Grouping is allowed, such as `Databases: PostgreSQL`, but an umbrella label must not imply unfamiliar products.

## Gaps

Gaps belong in the match report or a candidate follow-up list, not the CV. A tailored CV must be a truthful selection and presentation of existing evidence, not a proposed ideal CV.
