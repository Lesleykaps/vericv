# Privacy Policy

VeriCV is designed to work from local files and pasted text by default. It does not require uploading CVs, job descriptions, or candidate profiles to a third-party service.

The skill should not browse, store, email, or share candidate information unless the user explicitly requests an authorized action. Users remain responsible for reviewing generated material before sharing it with employers or other third parties.

The VeriCV repository contains no candidate CVs, job descriptions, or private credentials. Issues and contributions should likewise omit personal or confidential information.
